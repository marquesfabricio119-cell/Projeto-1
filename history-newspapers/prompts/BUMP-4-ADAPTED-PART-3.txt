=== RULES FOR EVERY PAGE ===
- FORMAT: vertical printable page, aspect ratio 2:3, portrait. The page fills the entire image edge to edge (NOT a mockup, no desk, no hands). It will be printed on US Letter paper. Safe margin about 5%.
- STYLE: large, clear, SANS-SERIF typography throughout (no serif fonts anywhere on these pages). Simple, friendly classroom design matching "World History Newspapers": light cream paper, deep wine red (#8A2B34) and antique gold (#C1902F) accents used sparingly, thick rounded boxes with soft dark outlines, generous white space. This material is for special education, autism support, English learners, and struggling readers, so keep every page UNCROWDED: large text, short lines, one idea per box, plenty of empty space. PRINTER-FRIENDLY: mostly white/cream space with dark text.
- ILLUSTRATION: on READ pages only, one large, warm, full-color painted illustration (museum-painting style, never a photo), filling about half the page, simple and easy to "read" at a glance — clear shapes, calm background, no small details that are hard to see.
- ICONS: small simple flat icons (not painted, bold outline, single flat color fill, one consistent icon style across the whole batch) next to the WHO/WHERE/WHEN boxes, the KEY WORDS, the CIRCLE THE ANSWER options, and the MATCH pictures.
- WRITING LINES: wherever the page says "lines", draw empty ruled lines. Leave them EMPTY.
- TEXT: American English, spelled EXACTLY as given. Copy the text; do not paraphrase, shorten or add words. Do NOT add mottos, slogans, quotes, extra headings, metaphors, or irony. No gibberish filler anywhere. Text must be large and sharp.
- Classroom-safe: no blood, no gore, no nudity. No anachronisms. War content is always shown gently and from a distance — no weapons in use, no combat, no visible harm to any person.
- FOOTER on every page: a thin dark bar with "World History Newspapers · Classroom Archive" and, in small italic on the right, "Adapted History · for classroom use". No other logos, no watermark.
